Disclaimer & Safety

Decision support, not a legal substitute for certified inspection

This app is a diagnostic assistant. It can help organize fault codes, symptoms, media clues, and first checks, but it does not guarantee a correct repair or replace manufacturer service information.

Audio, camera, photo, and video analysis are evidence-based cues only. They are not laboratory-grade measurements and should never be the sole basis for safety-critical repair decisions.

Without a compatible vehicle interface, the app cannot directly read live ECU data from the OBD port. Manual code entry and media-assisted triage do not fully replace a physical scan tool for live data, readiness monitors, OEM functions, or bidirectional controls.

Stop using the vehicle and seek qualified service immediately for brake failure, steering failure, fuel leaks, severe overheating, oil-pressure warnings, battery thermal events, or heavy engine knock.
