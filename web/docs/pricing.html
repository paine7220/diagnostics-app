Suggested Pricing & Fees

Editable launch model for a one-time-fee app

Suggested consumer launch model:

  • Base app: one-time purchase
  • No subscription by default
  • Optional future pro tools: one-time upgrade instead of recurring billing

Suggested launch price bands:

  • Budget: $4.99 to $7.99
  • Main launch: $9.99 to $14.99
  • Higher-trust release with broader support/docs: $19.99+

Developer-side platform costs to remember:

  • Apple Developer Program fee (or Microsoft Store / other store registration fees)
  • Store service fees for digital sales if you use in-app digital billing flows
  • Your own hosting, support, and maintenance costs

For physical services or physical goods, the billing rules are different from purely digital in-app items. Review current store policy before launch.
