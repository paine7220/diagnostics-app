Privacy

When Dexcom Share or Nightscout is connected, glucose readings are fetched over the network (Share goes through your alert worker proxy). Family alert SMS goes through the webhook you configure.

Care notes and medications stay in this device’s browser storage unless you export a backup.

Family phone numbers you enter are included in that alert payload so the webhook can deliver SMS.

Import notes only uses text you paste. Clearing site data or Erase all data removes the local copy.
