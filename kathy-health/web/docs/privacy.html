Privacy policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Kathy Health is designed to keep health information on your device.

  • We do not create accounts.
  • We do not upload medications, symptoms, vitals, or notes to a Kathy Health server.
  • Data is stored locally in the app / browser storage on your iPhone.
  • Optional export creates a file you choose to save or share.
  • If you clear app data or reinstall without a backup, your records are gone.

If this app is distributed through the App Store by Michael Paine, contact the publisher via the App Store listing for privacy questions.
